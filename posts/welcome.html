Welcome to My Blog

December 15, 2024 Personal 3 min read

Welcome to my personal blog! I'm Dylan Fernandez de Lara, and I'm excited to share this space with you.

This blog will serve as a platform where I can share my thoughts, experiences, and insights across the various facets of my life - from my work as a systems engineer at Meta Reality Labs to my musical journey and everything in between.

What You Can Expect

Here's what I plan to write about:

  • Technology & Engineering: Deep dives into systems engineering, crash intelligence, and the fascinating world of AR/VR development
  • Music & Performance: Reflections on my experiences with the National Youth Orchestra, Yale Symphony Orchestra, and how music has shaped my approach to problem-solving
  • Career & Growth: Lessons learned from Yale, transitioning into tech, and navigating the early stages of a career in systems engineering
  • Personal Reflections: Life in NYC, balancing technical and creative pursuits, and thoughts on where technology and the arts intersect

Why I'm Writing

I believe in the power of sharing knowledge and experiences. Whether you're a fellow engineer, a musician, a student, or someone who's simply curious about the intersection of technology and creativity, I hope you'll find something valuable here.

Writing also helps me process my own thoughts and experiences. In a world that moves as fast as tech does, taking time to reflect and articulate ideas feels more important than ever.

Let's Connect

I'd love to hear from you! Whether you want to discuss a post, share your own experiences, or just say hello, don't hesitate to reach out. The best conversations happen when different perspectives come together.

Thanks for stopping by, and I hope you'll join me on this journey!

Best,
Dylan
