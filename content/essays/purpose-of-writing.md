---
title: "On Writing"
date: "2025-12-20"
excerpt: "I would like to balance freedom of thought with organized ideas, so that writing lightens my spirit"
---

I have decided to start writing with three goals in mind:

1. Most of what I produce is in the engineering context and is written with brevity. I am human and feel the complexity of emotion, and I want to become better at turning non-technical thoughts into prose.
2. Writing is not algorithmic, and I struggle with the choosing what to write about, and the scope of what to write about. I would like to organize these ideas, so that writing becomes an enjoyable, honest reflection of myself and nurtures my soul.
3. I have always captured my life through vlogs, and I would like to add another dimension to this through writing and documenting the things I build.

\- DFDL
