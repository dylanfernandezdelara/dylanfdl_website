title: "The Purpose of Writing"
date: "2025-12-20"
excerpt: "I would like to balance freedom of thought with organized ideas, so that writing lightens my spirit"
---

I have decided to start writing with three goals in mind:

1. Most of what I produce is in the engineering context and is written with brevity. I am human and feel the complexity of emotion, and I want to become better at turning those thoughts into prose.
2. Writing is not algorithmic, and I struggle with the unlimited range of free writing expression. I would like to balance freedom of thought with organized ideas, so that writing lightens my spirit.
3. I have always captured my life through vlogs, and I want to add another dimension to this by writing, and documenting the things I build.

\- DFDL
